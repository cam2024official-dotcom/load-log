      Loaders

      Signing in on your phone
      1. The first time you open the app, tap your name and enter your PIN. The phone stays signed in after that.
      2. On a shared phone, open the menu and tap Switch person at the bottom. Pick your name and enter your own PIN.
      3. Not on the list or forgot your PIN? Ask a lead to add you or set a new one.
      Finding and opening a load
      1. Each order on the Load Log is one row: trailer or customer, loader, gallons and product, and ticket number. The colored bar on the left is the loader's color.
      2. Tap a row to open it. Tap it again to close it.
      3. Looking for one load? Type part of the trailer, ticket, BOL or loader name in the search box above the list. If only one load matches, it opens. Tap Clear to see them all again.
      4. When you're signed in, your own loads sit first under Your loads: the one you're loading, any finished ones still missing acid or water, then the ones waiting on you. The top one opens by itself.
      5. The right side shows the leave-by time, or the time it finished. While a load is going, the row shows a running timer.
      Starting a load
      1. Open the order and tap Start load. The timer starts.
      2. If no loader is named yet, your name is filled in. You can change it in the Loader box.
      3. Started the wrong one? Open Details at the bottom of the load and tap Undo start.
      4. Loads that need water have a blue water timer button. Tap it when the water starts. It counts down the water gallons ÷ 75 gpm, minus 1 minute so you have time to get back to the water tower. The countdown also shows on the load's row, and your phone buzzes when the water should be done. Water done before the timer, for example because the timer was started late? Tap the timer twice and it ends right away. The status shows Water finished. Started it by mistake? Tap Cancel timer twice.
      Ending a load and the load weight window
      1. Press and hold Hold to end load for about half a second, until the bar fills. The load weight window opens. A quick tap won't end it, so a slip or pocket tap can't end a load early.
      2. Enter the light weight (scale in) and heavy weight (scale out). Check the product is right.
      3. The window works out the gallons loaded. Water loaded fills in with the ticket water, since the water meter is right. If the load was cut for weight, it fills in the water for the cut gallons instead. Acid loaded gets the rest, since the acid tower runs a little short or over. For CableShield, the exact chemicals from the ticket are taken out first.
      4. Change the acid or water numbers if they don't match what you pulled, then tap Save amounts.
      5. No scale ticket yet? Tap Later. You can open the window again from the finished load with Figure from scale weights.
      Entering acid and water by hand
      1. Open the finished load and type the gallons in Acid loaded and Water loaded. It saves as you type.
      2. Acid is needed on every acid load. Water is needed on diluted acid and CableShield loads.
      3. Until they're filled in, the load shows "Needs acid" (or water) and the box is marked in yellow.
      The crew board
      1. The tiles at the top show each loader and what they're loading right now, with a live timer.
      2. Tap a name to jump to that loader's load.
      3. A yellow "Needs acid · Ticket ..." line means a finished load is missing amounts. Tap it to go straight to the empty box.
      4. The line under the counts shows the total acid pulled this shift and how many finished loads are still missing acid.
      Weight check (Texas, NM and Third Party)
      1. Texas loads can't go over 84,000 lb, and the target is 83,000. NM loads have a yellow NM tag and Third Party loads have a purple 3P tag. Both can't go over 80,000 lb, and the target is 79,000.
      2. Before loading, open the load, tap Weight check and enter the scale-in light weight. It shows the projected weight of the order and how many gallons fit.
      3. If the whole order fits, it says so. Load the amounts in Needs. When the order is too heavy, Needs switches to the cut-back amounts and turns orange, so you don't have to open Weight check to see them.
      4. Halliburton chems are counted in the weight at the product's weight, and they are never cut. Only the product gallons come down.
      Fixing a mistake
      1. Edit and Remove are under Details at the bottom of the load, with the loader, Delivering to and On loadout sheet. Edit changes the trailer, product, gallons, times, ticket, BOL and Halliburton chems.
      2. Reopen puts a finished load back to loading.
      Acid Dilution and Load Weight pages
      1. Acid Dilution: pick the product and strength, enter total gallons, and it shows how much acid, water and chemicals go in.
      2. Load Weight: works like the load weight window, without tying it to an order. Enter light and heavy weights, the product, and the Total Gallons Requested from the ticket. It shows gallons loaded, water from the ticket, and acid as the rest (CableShield chemicals come off first). Product weights can be changed at the bottom of that page.
      Crew Chat
      1. Open the menu and tap Crew Chat. A red number next to it, or a red dot on the menu button, means new messages.
      2. Type in the box at the bottom and tap Send. Your name and the time go on it for you.
      3. Messages delete themselves 24 hours after they're sent. Everyone on the crew can read them, so keep private things out.
      4. Tap Delete on your own message to take it down. Leads can delete any message.
      5. The phone doesn't buzz for new messages while the app is closed. You'll see them next time you open it.
      The schedule
      1. Open the menu and tap Schedule to see who's on day and night shift this week.
      2. Day shift is 6 AM to 6 PM and night shift is 6 PM to 6 AM, unless a lead changes the hours.
      3. Only people on shift show on the crew board and in the loader list. Anyone already on a load still shows.
      4. Covering a shift and your name isn't on the sign-in screen? Tap Show everyone, or ask a lead to mark you on shift.
      Who's online
      1. A green dot next to a name on the crew board means that person has the app open right now.
      2. Under the Live line, Online now lists everyone with the app open and which page they're on.
      3. Someone drops off when they close the app, put the phone down, or lose signal for a few minutes.
      Ask Tarquino
      1. Tap the round Tarquino button at the bottom right of the Load Log, or open the menu and tap Ask Tarquino. Type a question and tap Send, or tap one of the examples.
      2. It answers gallons loaded from light and heavy weights, acid and water for any gallons and product, heel from the scale light weight and the system light weight, how much fits for Texas, NM or Third Party, what gallons weigh, and lb per gallon.
      3. It also answers about the board: what's loading or waiting, loads done and acid pulled this shift, who's on shift, and finding a trailer or ticket. Ask about a load by trailer or ticket, like "how much fits on trailer 707", "what does 880 need", "heel on 880 lw 35800 system 32800" or "gallons loaded on 707 hw 76000". It uses that load's product, gallons, light weight, chems, heel and state. Follow up with "what about 15%", "make it 4000 gal", "and for NM" or "lw 34000 instead" and it reuses your last question.
      4. He can change a load for you after you tap Confirm: "set lw 34000 on 707", "set hw 76000 on 707", "put a 347 gal 20% heel on 880", "remove the heel on 880", "start 707", "assign 707 to Tony", "mark 880 third party".
      5. Leads can add an order by message: "add trailer 512, 5000 gal 15%, ticket 19131, NM, leave by 3pm, Tony". If something's missing he asks for it, and you can just send it ("5000 gal of 15%"). He shows the order and adds it when you tap Confirm. Add a heel or Halliburton chems afterward with Edit.
      6. Ask about time: "how long has 707 been loading", "what's late", "what's due in the next 2 hours", "average load time this shift". And about the latest turnover: "how many Y007 totes", "east tank sonar", "what equipment is down", "rail cars".
      7. Tap the microphone to say your question instead of typing. It uses your phone's voice typing, which usually needs a signal. "How do I…" questions are answered from this Help page. The chat stays while the app is open and clears when you close it.
      8. Name the product in the question, like "of 20%", "CableShield 100%" or a saved recipe name. Answers use the same charts and weights as the rest of the app and work without a signal.
      Shift handoff
      1. Open the menu and tap Shift Handoff to read the last Loader Shift Turnover: trailers being loaded, loaders on shift, rail cars, transloader, blend station and tank battery levels, tote counts, maintenance concerns and notes.
      2. When a lead posts a turnover, a message also goes in Crew Chat.
      3. Tap Print or save PDF on a turnover for a copy laid out like the paper sheet.
      4. Turnovers from the last 7 days are kept under Earlier turnovers.

      Leads

      Adding and removing orders
      1. Tap Add an order on the Load Log. Fill in the trailer or customer, product, ticket gallons, ticket number and anything else you have, then tap Save order.
      2. Blended order, like a CableShield batch cut with water? Pick Custom blend under Product. Enter a blend name, the base batch product and gallons, and the water added after. Needs shows the water, acid and chemicals for the whole load. Tap Save as recipe to keep it: next time pick it under Saved recipe and it scales to the total gallons. If the load is too heavy, every part is cut back evenly.
      3. Trailer came back with product in it? Tick Heel?, pick the heel product and enter its gallons. Its acid and water come off what the load needs, and the weight check counts it as already in the trailer.
      4. Halliburton load? Tick Halliburton chems and fill in up to 3 chems, each with its name and amount in gallons. They show on the order and are a record only. They don't change the acid and water math.
      5. Pick New Mexico or Third Party under Delivering to for those loads. Both have the 80,000 lb limit.
      6. To remove one, open it, tap Details at the bottom, then Remove. Only leads see these buttons.
      Management logins
      1. In the Lead Panel, under Management, type the manager's name and a PIN, then tap Add.
      2. They sign in on their phone by picking their name under Management on the sign-in screen and entering their PIN.
      3. Managers have full lead access: adding, editing and removing loads, the Lead Panel, the turnover and everything else. They aren't on the crew board, the schedule or the loader lists, and starting a load doesn't make them the loader.
      4. Change a manager's PIN or remove them from the same list.
      Adding people and setting PINs
      1. Open the Lead Panel. Under Crew Roster, type the name as it should print, add a PIN, tick Lead if they're a lead, and tap Add.
      2. To give someone a new PIN, tap Set PIN or Change PIN next to their name.
      3. Leads get into the Lead Panel and can remove loads without typing the lead PIN.
      4. Removing someone from the roster signs them out on their phone.
      Filling out the shift handoff
      1. Open Shift Handoff. Under This shift is the Loader Shift Turnover sheet. Fill it in as the shift goes. It saves as you type.
      2. Fill from Load Log puts in the trailers loading now, or the number of trailers finished this shift. Fill from Schedule puts in who is on shift. You can change anything after.
      3. At shift change, tap Post turnover twice. That signs it as the outgoing supervisor. The next lead opens it and taps Sign as incoming.
      4. South rail cars, transloader, levels, tote counts and maintenance concerns not yet reported carry over for the next shift to update. The rest starts fresh.
      Setting the schedule
      1. Open Schedule from the menu. Tap a day under someone's name to switch it between Day, Night and Off. It repeats every week.
      2. Someone with no days set always shows, so nothing changes until you set their days.
      3. Tap Mark on shift or Mark off shift to override the schedule. It clears at the end of the current shift. Tap Follow schedule to clear it sooner.
      4. To change the shift start times, open Shift hours at the bottom of the Schedule page.
      Loader colors
      1. In the Lead Panel, tap a color swatch next to a name. That color shows on their load rows and crew tile.
      Paperwork
      1. Print loadout sheet builds the sheet from the loads marked "On loadout sheet."
      2. Copy shift log gives a table you can paste into a spreadsheet.
      3. Clear finished orders removes done loads when the shift is wrapped up. Tap it twice to confirm.
      The backup lead PIN
      1. The crew's backup lead PIN opens the Lead Panel and removes loads on any phone, even one signed in as a loader. Keep it to leads.
