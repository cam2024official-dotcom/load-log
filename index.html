          Loaders

          Signing in on your phone
          1. The first time you open the app, tap your name and enter your PIN. The phone stays signed in after that.
          2. On a shared phone, open the menu and tap Switch person at the bottom. Pick your name and enter your own PIN.
          3. Not on the list or forgot your PIN? Ask a lead to add you or set a new one.
          Finding and opening a load
          1. Each order on the Load Log is one row: trailer or customer, loader, gallons and product, and ticket number. The colored bar on the left is the loader's color.
          2. Tap a row to open it. Tap it again to close it.
          3. Looking for one load? Type part of the trailer, ticket, BOL or loader name in the search box above the list. If only one load matches, it opens. Tap Clear to see them all again.
          4. When you're signed in, your own loads sit first under Your loads: the one you're loading, any finished ones still missing acid or water, then the ones waiting on you. The top one opens by itself.
          5. The right side shows the leave-by time, or the time it finished. While a load is going, the row shows a running timer.
          Starting a load
          1. Open the order and tap Start load. The timer starts.
          2. If no loader is named yet, your name is filled in. You can change it in the Loader box. Managers and dispatch logins are never filled in as the loader.
          3. Started the wrong one? Open Details at the bottom of the load and tap Undo start.
          4. Loads that need water have a blue water timer button. Tap it when the water starts. It counts down the water gallons ÷ 75 gpm, minus 5 minutes so you have time to get back to the water tower. The countdown also shows on the load's row, and your phone buzzes when the water should be done. Water done before the timer, for example because the timer was started late? Tap the timer twice and it ends right away. The status shows Water finished. Started it by mistake? Tap Cancel timer twice.
          Pausing a load
          1. Have to leave a load to tend to something else? Open it and tap Pause load. The load clock stops, and so does a running water timer, so shut the water off before you walk away. Pick why if you want (Another load, Truck/driver, Equipment, Break or Other). You can skip it.
          2. Back on it? Tap Resume load. The clock and the water timer pick up where they left off. You can still hold to end the load while it's paused.
          3. Paused loads show a yellow PAUSED tag on the board and the crew board. The paused time is left out of the load time, and the Shift Report, History and Turnover show the pauses. You can also tell Tarquino "pause 707 for equipment" or "resume 707".
          Inspecting your truck
          1. When a lead gives you a truck for the shift, it shows at the top of the Load Log. Tap Inspect.
          2. Mark each point OK or Problem, or tap All OK if everything checks out. If something's wrong, say what in the notes. Then tap Save inspection.
          3. The strip turns green when it's done (red if you reported a problem), and your lead sees it on the Daily Checklist. You can inspect a forklift or other equipment from the same window by picking it in the Equipment box.
          Yard notes
          1. Yard notes at the top of the Load Log is this shift's to-do list from the lead's walk around the yard. Tap the heading to fold it up or open it.
          2. Took care of one? Tap it to check it off. It records your name and the time. Tap again if you checked the wrong one.
          3. Leads: type what you see in the box and tap Add (or press Enter). × removes a note. The list starts fresh each shift.
          Dispatcher view
          1. For dispatch: a lead turns on Dispatcher view for you under Permissions. Leads have it too.
          2. Tap Dispatcher view at the top of the Load Log. The board is swapped for one list of every load on the board and in History, by shift: status, loader, product, ticket gallons, ticket, BOL, light weight (LW), heavy weight (HW), gallons loaded from the scale, acid and water loaded, times and leave-by. Each shift has totals.
          3. Pick a shift or all 7 days, or search by trailer, ticket, BOL, loader or product. Copy for spreadsheet copies what's shown to paste into Excel or an email.
          4. It's view only. Tap the switch again to go back to the normal board. Your phone remembers which one you had on.
          Shop TV board
          1. For the office desktop or a TV: open the menu and tap Shop TV board. It fills the screen with Loading now, Waiting (in the order the lead set), Done this shift, and Railcars, Yard notes and Crew on shift, with a big clock.
          2. It updates live, and long columns scroll by themselves. Tap Full screen in the top corner to hide the browser bar. Press Esc or tap Exit to go back.
          3. To open straight into it, bookmark the app's address with #tv on the end. The computer needs the crew PIN entered once, like a phone. It's view only: nothing can be changed from the board.
          Railcar timer
          1. Starting to offload a railcar into the tank? Tap + Start railcar under the Loading / Waiting / Done boxes on the Load Log. Enter the car number, pick the tank, and set how long it takes (tap 3 h or type hours and minutes). Tap Start timer.
          2. Everyone sees the countdown. When time's up the car turns orange, every phone shows a message, and the phone that started it (and leads' phones) buzz. Need longer? Tap +30 min, or Change to set the time left.
          3. When the car's finished, tap Done, then Car empty, or Stopped, not empty if it has to be picked back up later. On the Shift Handoff, Fill from Load Log fills South rail car status and Rail cars moved to empty line from these.
          Ending a load and the load weight window
          1. Press and hold Hold to end load for about half a second, until the bar fills. The load weight window opens. A quick tap won't end it, so a slip or pocket tap can't end a load early.
          2. Enter the light weight (scale in) and heavy weight (scale out). Check the product is right.
          3. The window works out the gallons loaded. Water loaded fills in with the ticket water, since the water meter is right. If the load was cut for weight, it fills in the water for the cut gallons instead. Acid loaded gets the rest, since the acid tower runs a little short or over. For CableShield, the exact chemicals from the ticket are taken out first.
          4. Change the acid or water numbers if they don't match what you pulled, then tap Save amounts.
          5. No scale ticket yet? Tap Later. You can open the window again from the finished load with Figure from scale weights.
          Entering acid and water by hand
          1. Open the finished load and type the gallons in Acid loaded and Water loaded. It saves as you type.
          2. Acid is needed on every acid load. Water is needed on diluted acid and CableShield loads.
          3. Until they're filled in, the load shows "Needs acid" (or water) and the box is marked in yellow.
          The crew board
          1. The tiles at the top show each loader and what they're loading right now, with a live timer.
          2. Tap a name to jump to that loader's load.
          3. A yellow "Needs acid · Ticket ..." line means a finished load is missing amounts. Tap it to go straight to the empty box.
          4. The line under the counts shows the total acid pulled this shift and how many finished loads are still missing acid.
          Weight check (Texas, NM and third party drivers)
          1. Texas loads can't go over 84,000 lb, and the target is 83,000. NM loads have a yellow NM tag. Loads hauled by a third party driver have a purple 3P tag. Both can't go over 80,000 lb, and the target is 79,000, wherever the load is going.
          2. Before loading, open the load, tap Weight check and enter the scale-in light weight. It shows the projected weight of the order and how many gallons fit.
          3. If the whole order fits, it says so. Load the amounts in Needs. When the order is too heavy, Needs switches to the cut-back amounts and turns orange, so you don't have to open Weight check to see them.
          4. Halliburton chems are counted in the weight at the product's weight, and they are never cut. Only the product gallons come down.
          Fixing a mistake
          1. Edit and Remove are under Details at the bottom of the load, with the loader, Delivering to and On loadout sheet. Edit changes the trailer, product, gallons, times, ticket, BOL and Halliburton chems.
          2. To change just the gallons, leave-by, ticket or BOL, open the load and tap that box at the top (the ones with ✎). Type the new one and tap Save.
          3. Trailer has a heel? Tap the wide Heel ✎ box, pick the heel product, enter its gallons and tap Save. Needs and the weight check update right away. Tap No heel to take it off.
          4. A load with notes shows a blue Note line on the list and a blue Special instructions box at the top when you open it. Read it before you start loading.
          5. Reopen puts a finished load back to loading.
          Acid Dilution and Load Weight pages
          1. Acid Dilution: pick the product and strength, enter total gallons, and it shows how much acid, water and chemicals go in.
          2. Load Weight: works like the load weight window, without tying it to an order. Enter light and heavy weights, the product, and the Total Gallons Requested from the ticket. It shows gallons loaded, water from the ticket, and acid as the rest (CableShield chemicals come off first). Product weights can be changed at the bottom of that page.
          Crew Chat
          1. Open the menu and tap Crew Chat. A red number next to it, or a red dot on the menu button, means new messages.
          2. Type in the box at the bottom and tap Send. Your name and the time go on it for you.
          3. Messages delete themselves 24 hours after they're sent. Everyone on the crew can read them, so keep private things out.
          4. Tap Delete on your own message to take it down. Leads can delete any message.
          5. The phone doesn't buzz for new messages while the app is closed. You'll see them next time you open it.
          The schedule
          1. Open the menu and tap Schedule to see who's on day and night shift this week.
          2. Day shift is 6 AM to 6 PM and night shift is 6 PM to 6 AM, unless a lead changes the hours.
          3. Only people on shift show on the crew board and in the loader list. Anyone already on a load still shows.
          4. Covering a shift and your name isn't on the sign-in screen? Tap Show everyone, or ask a lead to mark you on shift.
          Who's online
          1. A green dot next to a name on the crew board means that person has the app open right now.
          2. Under the Live line, Online now lists everyone with the app open and which page they're on.
          3. Someone drops off when they close the app, put the phone down, or lose signal for a few minutes.
          Ask Tarquino
          1. Tap the round Tarquino button at the bottom right of the Load Log, or open the menu and tap Ask Tarquino. Type a question and tap Send, or tap one of the examples.
          2. It answers gallons loaded from light and heavy weights, acid and water for any gallons and product, heel from the scale light weight and the system light weight, how much fits for Texas, NM or Third Party, what gallons weigh, and lb per gallon.
          3. It also answers about the board: what's loading or waiting, loads done and acid pulled this shift, who's on shift, and finding a trailer or ticket. Ask about a load by trailer or ticket, like "how much fits on trailer 707", "what does 880 need", "heel on 880 lw 35800 system 32800" or "gallons loaded on 707 hw 76000". It uses that load's product, gallons, light weight, chems, heel and state. Follow up with "what about 15%", "make it 4000 gal", "and for NM" or "lw 34000 instead" and it reuses your last question.
          4. He can change a load for you after you tap Confirm: "set lw 34000 on 707", "set hw 76000 on 707", "put a 347 gal 20% heel on 880", "remove the heel on 880", "start 707", "assign 707 to Tony", "mark 880 third party", "BOL 88120 on 707", "ticket 4412 on 707", "leave by 3:30 pm on 707", "set 707 to 4500 gal", "note on 707: top off only", "clear notes on 707", "end 707".
          5. Ask "notes on 707" or "any special instructions?" to read notes, and "what's the BOL on 707" for its ticket, BOL, leave-by and heel.
          6. Schedule: "who's on tonight", "who's off tomorrow", "when do I work next", "is Tony on Saturday night", "my schedule". History (for people who can see the Shift Report): "what did 707 load last time", "how many loads yesterday", "acid pulled this week", "how many loads did Tony do today". History keeps 7 days.
          7. Leads can add an order by message: "add trailer 512, 5000 gal 15%, ticket 19131, NM, leave by 3pm, Tony". If something's missing he asks for it, and you can just send it ("5000 gal of 15%"). He shows the order and adds it when you tap Confirm. You can put a heel, BOL and notes in the same message: "…, BOL 88120, 300 gal 20% heel, note top off only" (put the note last). Add Halliburton chems afterward with Edit.
          8. Ask about time: "how long has 707 been loading", "what's late", "what's due in the next 2 hours", "average load time this shift". And about the latest turnover: "how many Y007 totes", "east tank sonar", "what equipment is down", "rail cars".
          9. He's got some personality too: say hi, thank him, ask how he's doing or ask for a joke. He answers like a yard hand who's been around a while, but the numbers always come straight from the charts.
          10. Tap the microphone to say your question instead of typing. It uses your phone's voice typing, which usually needs a signal. "How do I…" questions are answered from this Help page. The chat stays while the app is open and clears when you close it.
          11. Name the product in the question, like "of 20%", "CableShield 100%" or a saved recipe name. Answers use the same charts and weights as the rest of the app and work without a signal.
          Shift handoff
          1. Open the menu and tap Shift Handoff to read the last Loader Shift Turnover: trailers being loaded, loaders on shift, rail cars, transloader, blend station and tank battery levels, tote counts, maintenance concerns and notes.
          2. When a lead posts a turnover, a message also goes in Crew Chat.
          3. Tap Print or save PDF on a turnover for a copy laid out like the paper sheet.
          4. Turnovers from the last 7 days are kept under Earlier turnovers.

          Leads

          Adding and removing orders
          1. Tap Add an order on the Load Log. Fill in the trailer or customer, product, ticket gallons, ticket and BOL numbers and anything else you have, then tap Save order.
          2. Same kind of load as one you just did? Tap one of the Repeat a recent load buttons at the top of the form. It fills in the product, gallons, state, third party and Halliburton chems. Then type the trailer and ticket.
          3. Tap +1 h, +2 h, +3 h or +4 h under Leave by to set it that far from now.
          4. BOL # goes next to Ticket #. Put special instructions in Notes / special instructions: they show in blue on the load so the loader sees them.
          5. Halliburton chems, the loadout sheet box and start and end times for a load that already happened are under More. When it's closed, the More bar shows anything filled in there.
          6. To add another load like one on the board, open it, tap Details, then Copy. Add order opens filled in from it, so you only type the new trailer, ticket and leave-by.
          7. J2 loads use the original CableShield batch (36% acid, water and Y007 only). Pick CableShield 100% or CableShield 33% and tick J2 load (original CableShield batch) under Product. Needs, the weight check, the load weight window and the reports then use the J2 chart, and the load shows a CS · J2 tag. You can also tick or untick it later under Edit.
          8. Blended order, like a CableShield batch cut with water? Pick Custom blend under Product. Enter a blend name, the base batch product and gallons, and the water added after. Needs shows the water, acid and chemicals for the whole load. Tap Save as recipe to keep it: next time pick it under Saved recipe and it scales to the total gallons. If the load is too heavy, every part is cut back evenly.
          9. Trailer came back with product in it? Tick Heel? under Third party driver, pick the heel product and enter its gallons. Found a heel after the order was added? Open the load and tap the Heel ✎ box under the other ✎ boxes. Its acid and water come off what the load needs, and the weight check counts it as already in the trailer.
          10. Halliburton load? Open More, tick Halliburton chems and fill in up to 3 chems, each with its name and amount in gallons. They show on the order and are a record only. They don't change the acid and water math.
          11. Pick New Mexico under Delivering to for NM loads. If a third party company's driver is hauling it, tick Third party driver. Both use the 80,000 lb limit. You can change either later under Details on the load.
          12. To remove one, open it, tap Details at the bottom, then Remove. Only leads see these buttons.
          Setting the order of waiting loads
          1. Tap Set order on the Waiting heading of the Load Log (leads, and anyone who can add orders).
          2. Use ▲ and ▼ to move a load up or down, or Top to put it first. It saves as you go and every phone sees it. Tap Done when you're finished.
          3. Waiting loads then show their place in line, and the first one shows NEXT. Tarquino, the Shift Handoff and the Shift Report use the same order. New orders go to the end, after the ones you placed, by leave-by time.
          4. Starting a load takes it out of line. Back to leave-by order clears the order you set.
          Shift Report
          1. Open the menu and tap Shift Report (leads and managers). Pick This shift, Last shift, Today, Last 24 h or 7 days.
          2. On 7 days, a Loads per day chart shows each day's loads split into day shift and night shift (a night shift counts toward the day it started). Hover over or tap a day for its gallons and acid; the table under the chart has the same numbers.
          3. It shows loads finished, gallons, acid and water loaded, average load time, late loads, loads cut for weight and missing amounts, then Loader stats (each loader's loads, gallons, average and fastest load time, how many went out on time, paused time, and how often they inspected their own truck), totals by product and destination, Halliburton chem totals, every finished load, and what's still open.
          4. Tap Print or save PDF for a paper copy. It prints in landscape on one page, with the text sized to fill the page: bigger on a quiet shift, smaller on a busy one.
          5. It includes finished loads in History, so it still works after Clear finished orders. Pick Earlier shift for any shift in the last 7 days.
          History
          1. Clear finished orders moves finished loads to History instead of deleting them. They're kept for 7 days.
          2. Open the menu and tap History (same people who can see the Shift Report). Loads are grouped by shift (day shift 6 AM to 6 PM, night shift 6 PM to 6 AM, filed under the day it started), with the number of loads and gallons for each. Search by trailer, ticket, BOL, loader or product, or pick a shift. Tap a load to see everything about it.
          3. Cleared one by mistake? Open it and tap Put back on the board (anyone with Paperwork access).
          4. Remove on a load still deletes it for good, since that's for mistakes.
          Permissions
          1. In the Lead Panel, under Permissions, tick what each crew member can do: Add orders, Remove orders, Turnover (fill in, post and sign the Shift Handoff), Schedule, Shift Report and Paperwork (copy shift log, print loadout sheet, clear finished orders).
          2. It saves right away and works on their phone as soon as it updates. Leads, managers and Admin always have everything.
          3. Someone with Paperwork opens the Lead Panel without the lead PIN but only sees the paperwork part. The crew list, PINs, management and permissions stay with leads.
          Management logins
          1. In the Lead Panel, under Management, type the manager's name and a PIN, then tap Add.
          2. They sign in on their phone by picking their name under Management on the sign-in screen and entering their PIN.
          3. Managers have full lead access: adding, editing and removing loads, the Lead Panel, the turnover and everything else. They aren't on the crew board, the schedule or the loader lists, and starting a load doesn't make them the loader.
          4. Change a manager's PIN or remove them from the same list.
          Adding people and setting PINs
          1. Open the Lead Panel. Under Crew Roster, type the name as it should print, add a PIN, tick Lead if they're a lead, and tap Add.
          2. To give someone a new PIN, tap Set PIN or Change PIN next to their name.
          3. Leads get into the Lead Panel and can remove loads without typing the lead PIN.
          4. Removing someone from the roster signs them out on their phone.
          Filling out the shift handoff
          1. Open Shift Handoff. Under This shift is the Loader Shift Turnover sheet. Fill it in as the shift goes. It saves as you type.
          2. Fill from Load Log puts in the trailers loading now, or the number of trailers finished this shift. Fill from Schedule puts in who is on shift. You can change anything after.
          3. At shift change, tap Post turnover twice. That signs it as the outgoing supervisor. The next lead opens it and taps Sign as incoming.
          4. South rail cars, transloader, levels, tote counts and maintenance concerns not yet reported carry over for the next shift to update. The rest starts fresh.
          Daily Checklist & Assignment
          1. Open the menu and tap Daily Checklist & Assignment under Lead. It starts fresh every shift.
          2. Shift checklist: tap Trailer List or any item to check it off. It records who and when. Turnover checks itself when the incoming lead signs the turnover, and Equipment Inspection checks itself once every assigned truck is inspected. Add one-off jobs under Special tasks this shift.
          3. Truck assignments: everyone on the schedule this shift is listed. Pick a truck for each loader. A truck can only go to one person. Same trucks as last time gives each loader the truck they had last.
          4. Equipment inspection shows every piece of equipment and whether it's been inspected, by whom and any problems. You can inspect from here too.
          5. Equipment use, last 7 days shows who had each truck and whether it was inspected, plus how often each loader inspected their own truck.
          6. Equipment list (at the bottom): add your trucks, forklifts and other equipment, mark something out of service, change the inspection points for each type, and add your own checklist items for every shift.
          7. Crew members who aren't leads can be given this page under Permissions (Daily checklist).
          Setting the schedule
          1. Open Schedule from the menu. Tap a day under someone's name to switch it between Day, Night and Off. It repeats every week.
          2. Someone with no days set always shows, so nothing changes until you set their days.
          3. Tap Mark on shift or Mark off shift to override the schedule. It clears at the end of the current shift. Tap Follow schedule to clear it sooner.
          4. To change the shift start times, open Shift hours at the bottom of the Schedule page.
          Loader colors
          1. In the Lead Panel, tap a color swatch next to a name. That color shows on their load rows and crew tile.
          Paperwork
          1. Print loadout sheet builds the sheet from the loads marked "On loadout sheet."
          2. Copy shift log gives a table you can paste into a spreadsheet.
          3. Clear finished orders removes done loads when the shift is wrapped up. Tap it twice to confirm.
          The backup lead PIN
          1. The crew's backup lead PIN opens the Lead Panel and removes loads on any phone, even one signed in as a loader. Keep it to leads.
